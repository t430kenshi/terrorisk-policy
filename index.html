Terrorisk Production

Internal production tooling for Terrorisk Works — not a public service.

What this is

Terrorisk Production is a private, single-user automation tool operated by the owner of the Terrorisk podcast. It handles the mechanical parts of episode production — media verification, edit specifications, quality-control evidence and document handoff between production roles.

Who can use it

One person: the account owner. There is no sign-up, no public interface and no third-party customer base. The application is not distributed, not offered as a service, and has no users other than its owner.

Google services

With the owner's explicit consent, the tool reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, and reads and writes Google Docs and Sheets, so that production documents can be exchanged with collaborators the owner has already authorised. It may also be granted access to the owner's Gmail, Calendar and Contacts for notification and scheduling tasks the owner requests.

Data

Data stays on equipment controlled by the owner. See the privacy policy for what is accessed, why, and how it is retained.
