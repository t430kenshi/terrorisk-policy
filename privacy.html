Privacy Policy

Terrorisk Production — last updated 3 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers a private, single-user production tool operated by the owner of the Terrorisk podcast (“the application”). It does not apply to the Terrorisk podcast itself, to any website, or to any third party.

2. What the application accesses

Only after the account owner grants consent, and only for the owner's own account:

  • Google Drive — to read and create production documents in folders the owner has shared with the application, and to verify that files it uploaded arrived intact.
  • Google Docs and Sheets — to read documentation and write production records.
  • Gmail — if granted, to send messages the owner explicitly requests and to read messages the owner explicitly asks it to review. It does not read mail in the background, does not build profiles, and does not send anything the owner has not requested.
  • Calendar and Contacts — if granted, to read or create the specific events and contact records the owner requests.

3. What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.
  • No data about anyone other than the consenting account owner.
  • No passwords. Authentication uses Google's OAuth 2.0; the application never sees the account password.

4. Storage and location

Working data is stored on the owner's own computer and on storage the owner controls. OAuth credentials are stored locally with file permissions restricted to the owner's system account. No production data is copied to any server operated by the application's author.

5. Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties. Documents are placed in Google Drive folders only where the owner has deliberately shared that folder with a collaborator, which is the normal and intended means of handoff within the owner's own production workflow.

6. Third-party AI processing

The application may send text to a large-language-model provider in order to perform the task the owner requests. It does not upload audio or video masters, credentials, or private media to external services.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data is retained until the owner deletes it. Drive documents remain until deleted by the owner. OAuth access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the application's access.

8. Security

Credentials are stored with owner-only file permissions and are not transmitted to third parties. Access is limited to a single machine and a single Google account.

9. Children

Not directed at children and not used by anyone under 13.

10. Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
